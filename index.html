The Bamfield Experience

Life on Canada’s Wild Pacific Coast

Bamfield is one of Vancouver Island’s most cherished coastal communities — remote enough to feel like an escape, vibrant enough to call home. Nestled at the entrance to Barkley Sound, it draws those who prize authentic West Coast living above all else.

01

West Coast Trail

The northern terminus of Canada’s iconic West Coast Trail sits minutes away — 75 kilometres of dramatic Pacific coastline through Pacific Rim National Park Reserve.

02

World-Class Fishing

Barkley Sound is legendary for chinook salmon, halibut, and lingcod. Whether by charter or your own vessel, the fishing here is exceptional throughout the season.

03

Barkley Sound

Kayaking, diving, whale watching, surfing — the Sound’s sheltered inlets and open ocean offer endless marine recreation for every skill level and season.

04

Marine Sciences Centre

The Bamfield Marine Sciences Centre is a world-renowned ocean research institution, lending the community an intellectual character unique among BC coastal villages.

05

Old-Growth Forest

Towering Douglas fir, red cedar, and Sitka spruce surround these lots, offering privacy and natural grandeur that has no equivalent in urban British Columbia.

06

A Real Community

A working marina, local services, and a warm village spirit — Bamfield is a genuine community, not a resort. Those who come here tend to stay.

Regional District of Alberni-Clayoquot

Zoning & Permitted Uses

All lots are designated under the RDAC Zoning Bylaw for acreage residential use, supporting low-density single-family development in rural settings and preserving the natural character of the area.

RA1 — Acreage Residential District 1

Single-Family Residential

Provides for single-family residential development on large lots in areas not ultimately intended for intensive urban development.

  • One single-family dwelling (owner-occupied)
  • Home occupations
  • Accessory buildings and uses
RA2 — Acreage Residential District 2

Single-Family Residential

Provides for single-family residential development on large lots in certain types of rural settings — the quintessential west coast acreage designation.

  • One single-family dwelling (owner-occupied)
  • Home occupations
  • Accessory buildings and uses

Servicing: Lots require individual septic systems and water wells.
