Why Here

Bamfield is today what Tofino was twenty years ago

For decades, Bamfield was the best-kept secret on Vancouver Island. Too remote for most, which kept it pristine. In 2023, the 76-kilometre road from Port Alberni was paved, opening Bamfield to year-round access without diminishing what makes it extraordinary.

Extraordinary Wildlife

Bald eagles nest overhead. Grey whales, humpbacks, and orca migrate through Barkley Sound. Steller sea lions haul out on nearby rocks. Roosevelt elk move through the forest edges at dawn. Wildlife here is not a highlight. It is daily life.

Old Growth Rainforest

The West Coast Trail passes nearby, threading through 77 kilometres of ancient Sitka spruce, western red cedar, and Douglas fir. The air smells of salt and cedar. The light filters green through canopies that have stood for centuries.

A Clear Investment Case

After the road was paved, average Bamfield house prices rose from $472,000 to $728,000. Western Investor named Bamfield one of BC's top five towns for real estate investment. The window to buy ahead of full discovery is narrow.

Barkley Sound

One of Vancouver Island's great marine environments. Kayaking, sport fishing, diving, and whale-watching are right outside your door. The Broken Group Islands, a world-class paddler's destination, lie minutes away by boat.

Bamfield Marine Sciences Centre

One of Canada's leading marine research institutions operates here year-round, drawing scientists from universities across the country. It gives Bamfield a uniquely thoughtful, engaged community unlike any other small coastal village.

True Scarcity

There are exactly 13 lots in Bamfield Sanctuary. There will never be more. Bounded by crown land and protected areas, this subdivision cannot expand. When these lots are sold, this opportunity is permanently closed.

Getting There

Closer than you think

Since the Bamfield Road was paved in 2023, getting here is easy and comfortable. From downtown Vancouver you can be standing in Bamfield in under five hours.

By Road via Port Alberni

Take BC Ferries from Tsawwassen to Nanaimo (Departure Bay), then drive the Island Highway south to Port Alberni. From there, the fully paved 76-kilometre Bamfield Road winds through old growth forest directly into the village. Straightforward and scenic.

4.5 to 5 hours from Vancouver, year-round access

By Ferry from Port Alberni

The MV Frances Barkley runs from Port Alberni to Bamfield through the Alberni Inlet, one of Vancouver Island's most spectacular coastal passages. The journey takes approximately 4.5 hours and is an experience in itself. A memorable way to arrive.

Seasonal schedule, check Lady Rose Marine Services

By Floatplane

Charter floatplane service is available from Vancouver Harbour or Victoria to Barkley Sound, landing directly in Bamfield. The fastest option from the Lower Mainland, and it offers an unforgettable first view of your future property from above the canopy.

Approximately 45 minutes from Vancouver Harbour
